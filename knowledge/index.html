Quick Reference: The Grillability Index™

Our proprietary scoring system combines all weather factors into a single score from 0-100. Here's how each factor contributes:

Temperature 35%

The most important factor. Extreme temperatures impact both cooking efficiency and your willingness to stand outside.

Precipitation 30%

Rain is a dealbreaker. Even light precipitation significantly impacts your grilling experience.

Wind 25%

Wind affects heat distribution and can make maintaining temperature nearly impossible.

Humidity 10%

Less critical but still affects your comfort, especially in extreme conditions.
